Text message alerts

South Dayton TOPSoccer sends occasional text messages to parents and guardians so you never miss a schedule change, a weather cancellation, or an event detail like picture day. Signing up is optional and takes one checkbox.

How to sign up

When you register your athlete on our online registration form, check the text alerts box and enter your mobile number. That's it — you're on the list. Consent is completely optional and is not required to register or participate.

What to expect

  • Who texts you: South Dayton TOPSoccer (a 501(c)(3) non-profit youth soccer program).
  • What we send: one-way program announcements only — schedule changes, weather cancellations, and event logistics. No marketing, no selling of anything.
  • How often: occasional — typically fewer than 10 messages per year.
  • Cost: message and data rates may apply, per your mobile carrier.
  • Opt out anytime: reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe. Reply HELP for help.
  • Your privacy: we never sell, rent, or share your mobile number or opt-in information with third parties.
Questions about our text program? Email admin@southdaytontopsoccer.com. Full details are in our SMS Privacy Policy and SMS Terms of Service.
